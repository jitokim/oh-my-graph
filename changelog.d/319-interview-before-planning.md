### Documented

ADR 0044 (Proposed) records the decision to offer an interview before planning, as an opt-in `auto --interview` and as an `oh-my-graph design` command that ends in a reviewed graph file, with `auto` unchanged when the flag is off. Nothing changes in behaviour yet: no flag, subcommand or schema key is added. ([#319](https://github.com/jitokim/oh-my-graph/issues/319))
